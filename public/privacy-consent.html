Privacy Policy & Data Consent

Last updated: May 2023

Introduction

Welcome to Code Guesser! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our game. We are committed to being transparent about our data practices and providing you with control over your information.

This game collects data to improve your experience and help us understand how the game is used. Some data collection is essential for the game to function properly, while other types require your explicit consent.

Types of Data We Collect

User Gameplay Data

Collected without explicit consent (essential for game functionality):

  • Number of games played
  • Game duration per session
  • Accuracy (correct vs. incorrect guesses)
  • Time taken per guess
  • Most frequently guessed tags/languages
  • Game abandonment rate
  • Snippet success rate

This data helps us balance difficulty, identify issues, and improve the overall game experience.

User Feedback & Reports

Collected with disclosure:

  • Feedback form responses
  • Mistake reports on snippets or tags

This information helps us improve the game's accuracy and quality.

Technical & Performance Data

Collected with disclosure (for game optimization):

  • Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)
  • Browser & OS version
  • Game load time & performance metrics
  • Crash logs & errors

This data helps us ensure the game runs smoothly across different devices and browsers.

User Profile & Behavioral Data

Requires your explicit consent:

  • User login & authentication data
  • Region/location (general, non-specific)
  • IP address (if stored for analytics)
  • Clickstream data (e.g., button clicks, page navigation)

This data helps us understand user behavior and improve the user experience.

How We Use Your Data

Essential Game Functions

  • To operate and maintain the game
  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To track game progress and scores
  • To provide the leaderboard functionality

Game Improvement

  • To understand how players interact with the game
  • To identify and fix bugs or issues
  • To optimize game performance
  • To improve code snippets and tag accuracy

With Your Consent

  • To analyze detailed usage patterns
  • To personalize your experience
  • To conduct research to improve our services

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have various rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • The right to access your data
  • The right to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to withdraw consent
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@codeguesser.com.

Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
